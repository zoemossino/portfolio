  1. 01Même pas sourd

    Activation — Petits Frères des Pauvres (school project)

    Même pas Sourd is a live intergenerational speed dating on Twitch, touring the concept all over France. Hang on and open your ears: the elders do not have their tongue in their pockets…

    The brief was to develop the notoriety of Petits Frères des Pauvres and (re)put the fight against the isolation of elderly people at the heart of concerns — by raising awareness and mobilising French citizens.

  2. 02La Patate Groove

    La Patate Douce Radio — fictional group project

    La Patate Douce Radio is a webradio born in Lyon (France) which broadcasts Disco-Funk Groovy, Afro-Soul & House 24/7.

    The project was to transform this application into a physical object or device. The Patate Groove is a mixing deck 100% connected to your La Patate Douce playlist — no need for USB!
